Device Setup

Connect your Cortex Band

Complete these steps once to pair your band with this checkout.

  1. 1

    Join setup network

    Power on the band, then connect your phone or laptop to the Wi‑Fi network Cortex‑CAM‑Setup.

  2. 2

    Choose your Wi‑Fi

    In the setup page that opens, select your campus Wi‑Fi network and enter its password.

  3. 3

    Wait for registration

    The band registers automatically. The status indicator turns green once it's connected.

If the setup page doesn't open automatically, visit 192.168.4.1 in your browser while connected to the setup network.
